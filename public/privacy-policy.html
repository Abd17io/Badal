Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Draft for review: This policy is based on Badal’s currently described features. Confirm it against the live app’s actual data collection, storage, service providers, and applicable legal requirements before publishing.

This Privacy Policy explains how Badal (“we,” “us,” or “our”) may collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the Badal application and related services. Badal helps people list items, discover items listed by others, and send and manage exchange requests.

By using Badal, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

In short: Badal processes information needed to manage accounts and listings, show items to other users, handle exchange requests, and operate and protect the service. Information you include in a listing may be visible to other users.

1. Information We May Collect

1.1 Account information

When you create or use an account, we may process information such as your name or display name, email address, account identifier, sign-in details, language choice, and other account settings. The exact information depends on the sign-in options and features available.

1.2 Listings and item information

When you post an item, we process the information you provide so that we can display and manage the listing. This may include item names in Arabic or English, descriptions, photos, category, transaction type, and listing status.

1.3 Exchange requests and app activity

We process information about exchange requests, including the items involved, request status, and actions such as accepting or declining an offer. We may also process information needed to provide in-app notifications and maintain your listing and request history.

1.4 Device and diagnostic information

The application or its service providers may receive technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, such as device and app version, language, IP address, connection details, and error or performance diagnostics. The information actually collected depends on the production configuration.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information for purposes such as:

  • Creating, authenticating, and managing user accounts.
  • Publishing, displaying, editing, and removing item listings.
  • Helping users discover items and filter listings.
  • Sending, receiving, and managing exchange requests and related notifications.
  • Providing support and responding to questions or requests.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, and improving the application.
  • Protecting users and the service, including detecting misuse and security incidents.
  • Meeting legal requirements and enforcing applicable terms.

3. What Other Users Can See

Item listings are intended to be discovered by other Badal users. The item name, photos, description, category, and transaction type you submit may therefore be shown to other users. When you send or receive an exchange request, relevant details about the request and the items involved may be shared with the other participant so they can review and respond.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in public listing fields or item photos. Review your listing before publishing it.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

We may allow trusted providers who help operate, host, maintain, secure, or support Badal to process information on our behalf. The providers and services used depend on the app’s actual production setup; their identities and roles should be confirmed before this policy is published.

We do not sell personal information. We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect users or our rights, or in connection with a business reorganization or transfer.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Badal, maintain account and listing functionality, handle exchange requests, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Retention periods may differ by information type and purpose.

You can manage or remove your listings through the app’s My Items area. To request account deletion or ask about information associated with your account, contact us using the details below. Some information may be retained when required by law or needed for legitimate security and recordkeeping purposes.

6. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict some processing. You can also choose what information you include in a listing and remove listings you no longer want to share.

To make a privacy request, contact us below. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfill a request in full where an exception under applicable law applies.

7. Children’s Privacy

Badal is not intended for children who are below the minimum age for using the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect children’s personal information in violation of those laws. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Security and International Processing

We use reasonable safeguards intended to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live, depending on where Badal and its service providers operate.

9. Third-Party Services

Badal may rely on third-party infrastructure or link to external services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. We will identify relevant providers here if they are used by the production application.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Badal, our data practices, or legal requirements change. We will update the date above when we make changes. Please review this page periodically.
